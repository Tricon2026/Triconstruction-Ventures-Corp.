Who we are

A dependable partner for construction supply.

Headquartered in Davao City, Triconstruction Ventures Corp. has provided quality construction materials and logistics support since its incorporation in 2013. We work with suppliers and customers to support reliable product availability and timely distribution.

We aim to maintain inventory readiness, support timely deliveries, provide dependable customer service, strengthen supplier relationships, and deliver consistent product quality.
