Company Overview

Triconstruction Ventures Corporation is a construction materials distribution company headquartered in Davao City, Philippines. Incorporated in 2013, the company supplies construction materials and supports distribution operations throughout Southern Mindanao.

With industry experience and relationships with suppliers, the company serves contractors, retailers, developers and other customers. Through organized operations, customer-focused service and distribution support, Triconstruction aims to be a dependable construction supply partner.
